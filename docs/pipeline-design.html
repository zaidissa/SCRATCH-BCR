SCRATCH-BCR design

10x 5′ VDJ-B reads in, one row of B-cell receptor metadata per cell out, keyed so it joins onto the SCRATCH-E2E gene-expression object next to the TCRtoolkit columns. The science follows nf-core/airrflow (Immcantation); the inputs, cell names and output bundle follow your existing pipelines.

How it merges with GEX and TCR

QC rules, in the order they are applied

Each contig gets the first reason it fails; contigs_before_qc.tsv records it in qc_fail_reason, so every dropped chain can be traced. A sample with no usable cells does not stop the run: it is marked no_contigs or no_cells_passed in bcr_qc_summary.tsv and named in a warning at the end.

  1. not_annotated: IgBLAST could not assign V and J against IMGT.
  2. non_productive: frameshift or stop codon (--bcr_productive_only).
  3. low_umi: fewer UMIs than --bcr_min_umis (1).
  4. multi_heavy: the cell has more than one productive IGH, usually a doublet (--bcr_drop_multi_heavy).
  5. no_heavy: light chain only (--bcr_require_heavy).
  6. extra_light or multi_light: more than one light chain. The default keeps the highest-UMI one; drop_cell removes the cell.
